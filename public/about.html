Our Purpose

VideoSnap is an independent web utility designed to help users save supported videos when they own the content or have permission from the rights holder. Results may vary depending on source availability, access permissions, and technical compatibility.

Please download only content you own or are authorized to save. Never use the service to access private content without permission or bypass access restrictions.

Our Values

  • Simplicity: keep the experience clear and easy to navigate.
  • Transparency: explain important service limitations honestly.
  • Responsible use: respect copyright, privacy, and applicable platform rules.
