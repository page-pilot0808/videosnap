1. Information We May Process

When you submit a video URL, VideoSnap may process that URL and technical information needed to handle your request. Depending on the hosting and server configuration, logs may include your IP address, request time, browser information, and error details.

Please do not submit confidential information or links to private content that you are not authorized to access.

2. How Information May Be Used

Technical information may be used to operate the website, respond to requests, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and maintain security. We do not claim to collect or use information beyond the practices actually enabled on the website.

3. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

The website may use essential technologies required for operation. If analytics or advertising services are added, this policy should be updated to identify the services actually used and explain their relevant data practices.

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize, limit, or measure advertisements, subject to applicable settings and policies. See Google's Advertising Policies and Technologies.

Before publishing, verify whether analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, or other tracking technologies are actually installed. Add any required consent options for the regions you serve.

4. Security and Data Retention

Technical logs should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, and service operation, subject to hosting configuration and applicable law. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

VideoSnap may rely on hosting infrastructure or interact with third-party video platforms. Those services have their own privacy policies and practices. Review the policies of any third-party service you use.

6. Children's Privacy

VideoSnap is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the site, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the service, hosting setup, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
