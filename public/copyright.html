1. Use Authorized Content

Users should download only videos they own or have permission to save. VideoSnap must not be used to infringe copyright, access private material without permission, or bypass access restrictions.

Having access to a video does not automatically give you permission to download, redistribute, edit, or commercially reuse it. Always check the rights and permissions that apply.

2. Report a Copyright Concern

If you believe that material or a feature associated with VideoSnap infringes your rights, contact us by email. Include the following information where applicable:

  • The URL of the page or material involved.
  • A description of the copyrighted work.
  • An explanation of your ownership or authorization to act for the rights holder.
  • Your name and contact information.
  • Any additional details needed to identify and review the concern.

3. Review and Action

We will review reports in good faith and take appropriate action where necessary and applicable. Depending on the circumstances, action may include restricting access to a feature or removing material within our control.

Please provide accurate information. Submitting a report does not guarantee a particular outcome or removal.

4. Copyright Contact

Send copyright-related concerns to the VideoSnap site operator.
