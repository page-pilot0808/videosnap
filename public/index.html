Quick questions 💬

Which platforms are supported?

VideoSnap is designed for supported YouTube and Facebook video links. Availability depends on the URL, platform changes, and server compatibility.

Will every video link work?

No. Private, restricted, removed, age-restricted, or otherwise inaccessible videos may not be available. Login-only videos and content that requires bypassing access controls are not supported.

Can I choose video quality?

Yes. When the server can retrieve the video's available formats, supported quality options will appear in the quality selector.

Can I use VideoSnap on my phone?

Yes. The page is designed to work with mobile phones, tablets, and desktop browsers. Download behavior may vary by browser and device.

Is there a download size limit?

The server is configured with a maximum download size of 500 MB. Actual availability also depends on hosting resources and the requested video's format.

Why is my video not loading?

Check that the URL is correct and that the video is accessible. Temporary server issues, platform changes, unsupported formats, or hosting timeouts may also cause a request to fail.

Is VideoSnap affiliated with YouTube or Facebook?

No. VideoSnap is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to YouTube, Google, Facebook, or Meta.
